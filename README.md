A one-star review of Monday, written as if Monday were a restaurant — and a
waiter who cannot get a single order right. The entry complains about cold
coffee, burnt toast, and the one thing no other day can take away from you:
the weekend, handed back as the bill.

> One star. The waiter Monday brought my coffee cold, my toast burnt, and my weekend back as the bill.

`main.ts` reads `entry.txt` at runtime, enforces the house rules (1–180
characters, not whitespace-only, at most 25 words), and writes the line to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`.
